SECTION 1: OVERVIEW
================================================================

PeakGear Pro stands behind the quality of every product we sell.
This document describes your warranty rights, what is covered,
how to make a claim, and our returns policy.

This policy applies to all PeakGear Pro branded products
purchased directly from PeakGear Pro or from an authorised
retailer. Proof of purchase is required for all claims.

================================================================
SECTION 2: WARRANTY COVERAGE PERIODS
================================================================

The following warranty periods apply from the original date
of purchase:

  HELMETS (all models)         — 3 years
  FOOTWEAR (all models)        — 1 year
  APPAREL (jerseys, shorts)    — 6 months
  ACCESSORIES (bags, bottles)  — 6 months
  ELECTRONICS (bike computers) — 1 year

Warranty coverage begins on the date shown on your proof of
purchase (receipt or order confirmation).

================================================================
SECTION 3: WHAT IS COVERED
================================================================

The PeakGear Pro warranty covers defects in materials and
workmanship under normal use and care. Examples include:

  - Helmet buckle or retention system failure not caused
    by impact or misuse
  - Sole delamination on footwear within the warranty period
  - Stitching failure on apparel under normal washing and use
  - Electronic component failure not caused by water damage
    or physical impact

================================================================
SECTION 4: WHAT IS NOT COVERED
================================================================

The warranty does not cover:

  - Normal wear and tear
  - Damage caused by accident, misuse, neglect, or impact
  - Damage caused by failure to follow care instructions
  - Products that have been modified or repaired by a
    third party
  - Cosmetic damage that does not affect performance
    (scratches, discolouration)
  - Helmets that have been involved in any impact event
    (these must be replaced regardless of visible damage)

================================================================
SECTION 5: HOW TO MAKE A WARRANTY CLAIM
================================================================

STEP 1 — GATHER YOUR INFORMATION
  You will need:
    - Proof of purchase (receipt or order confirmation email)
    - Product name and model number (on the product label)
    - Description of the defect
    - Photographs showing the defect clearly

STEP 2 — CONTACT PEAKGEAR PRO SUPPORT
  Submit your claim through one of the following channels:
    - Online: www.peakgearpro.example.com/warranty-claim
    - Email: warranty@peakgearpro.example.com
    - Phone: 0800 PEAKGEAR (Mon–Fri, 09:00–17:00 GMT)

STEP 3 — ASSESSMENT
  Our team will review your claim within 5 business days.
  We may request that you return the product for inspection.
  Return postage for warranty claims is covered by PeakGear Pro.

STEP 4 — RESOLUTION
  Approved warranty claims will be resolved by one of the
  following, at PeakGear Pro's discretion:
    - Replacement with the same or equivalent product
    - Repair of the defective product
    - Store credit to the value of the original purchase price

================================================================
SECTION 6: RETURNS POLICY
================================================================

CHANGE OF MIND RETURNS
Items may be returned within 60 days of purchase for a full
refund provided they are:
  - Unused and in original condition
  - In original packaging with all tags attached
  - Accompanied by proof of purchase

DEFECTIVE PRODUCT RETURNS
Defective products may be returned at any time within the
warranty period. See Section 5 for the claims process.

SIZING EXCHANGES
Unworn footwear and apparel may be exchanged for a different
size within 60 days of purchase. The item must be in original
condition with tags attached.

HOW TO RETURN AN ITEM
  1. Contact support to obtain a Return Merchandise
     Authorisation (RMA) number.
  2. Pack the item securely and include the RMA number
     on the outside of the package.
  3. Post to: PeakGear Pro Returns, Unit 4 Riverside
     Industrial Park, Bristol, BS1 4QR, United Kingdom.

Return postage costs are the customer's responsibility for
change of mind returns. PeakGear Pro covers postage for
defective product returns.
