Globex Corporation New Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Globex! This guide will help you get started in your first week.

Day 1: Orientation
- Report to the front desk at 9:00 AM.
- Collect your badge, laptop, and welcome kit.
- Complete HR paperwork (tax forms, direct deposit, emergency contacts).
- Attend the 1-hour company overview presentation.
- Meet your assigned buddy for lunch.

Day 2-3: Systems Setup
- Set up your email and Slack account.
- Complete required security training modules (estimated 2 hours).
- Request access to team-specific tools via the IT portal at it.globex.internal.
- Set up your development environment following the Engineering Wiki.

Day 4-5: Team Integration
- Shadow a teammate through their daily workflow.
- Attend your first team standup.
- Review the team's current sprint board and backlog.
- Schedule 1:1 meetings with key collaborators.

First 30 Days
- Complete all compliance training modules.
- Deliver your first small contribution (bug fix, documentation update, etc.).
- Attend the monthly all-hands meeting.
- Have your first formal 1:1 with your manager to set 90-day goals.

Key Contacts
- HR: hr@globex.com or ext. 2100
- IT Help Desk: helpdesk@globex.com or ext. 2200
- Facilities: facilities@globex.com or ext. 2300
- Your Buddy: Assigned on Day 1
