Globex Corporation Employee Benefits Guide 2024

Health Insurance
All full-time employees are eligible for health insurance starting on their first
day of employment. We offer three plan tiers: Basic, Standard, and Premium.
- Basic: $50/month, $3,000 deductible, covers preventive care and emergencies.
- Standard: $150/month, $1,500 deductible, covers specialist visits and prescriptions.
- Premium: $300/month, $500 deductible, comprehensive coverage including dental and vision.

Retirement Benefits
Globex offers a 401(k) plan with employer matching up to 6% of your salary.
Vesting is immediate for employee contributions. Employer match vests over 3 years:
- Year 1: 33% vested
- Year 2: 66% vested
- Year 3: 100% vested

Paid Time Off
- Vacation: 15 days per year for the first 3 years, 20 days after 3 years, 25 days after 7 years.
- Sick leave: 10 days per year, can roll over up to 30 days.
- Parental leave: 12 weeks fully paid for all new parents.
- Bereavement: 5 days for immediate family.

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote positions are available for certain roles. A $500 annual stipend
is provided for home office equipment.
