Annual Leave Policy

All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accruing at a rate of 1.67 days per month from their start date. Part-time employees receive a prorated entitlement based on their contracted hours. Annual leave is intended to provide employees with time to rest and recharge, and we encourage everyone to take their full entitlement each year to maintain a healthy work-life balance.

Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for periods of one week or less, and at least four weeks in advance for periods longer than one week. Managers will respond to leave requests within three business days. Approval is subject to team coverage requirements and business needs, and we recommend discussing extended leave plans with your manager informally before submitting a formal request.

Unused annual leave can be carried over to the next calendar year up to a maximum of 5 days. Any carried-over leave must be used by March 31 of the following year or it will be forfeited. In exceptional circumstances such as extended illness or critical business needs that prevented you from taking leave, additional carry-over may be approved by your department head in writing. Employees are not permitted to receive payment in lieu of untaken leave except upon termination of employment.

During your first year of employment, leave accrues from your start date and is available to use after completing your probationary period of 90 days. If you need to take time off during your probation period due to illness or personal emergency, speak with your manager and HR representative to discuss options. Upon termination of employment, any accrued but unused leave will be paid out in your final paycheck, calculated at your regular daily rate.

We observe a blackout period during the last two weeks of December due to year-end business activities. Leave requests during this period require director-level approval and will only be granted in exceptional circumstances. Outside of blackout periods, we aim to be as flexible as possible with leave scheduling and encourage employees to plan their time off early in the year to ensure their preferred dates are available.

Remote Work Policy

Our remote work policy reflects our commitment to flexible working arrangements while maintaining team collaboration and productivity. Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week with their manager's prior approval. The specific remote days should be agreed upon with your manager and should consider team meeting schedules, collaboration needs, and client-facing responsibilities.

Before beginning a remote work arrangement, employees must complete and sign a Remote Work Agreement, which outlines expectations, responsibilities, and the terms of the arrangement. This agreement covers areas including working hours, communication expectations, equipment use, data security, and workspace requirements. The agreement is reviewed annually and can be modified by either party with two weeks written notice.

Employees working remotely must be available and responsive during core hours of 10 AM to 3 PM in their local time zone. Outside of core hours, employees may structure their remaining work hours flexibly as long as they complete their full contracted hours each day and attend any scheduled meetings. Remote workers are expected to maintain the same level of productivity and quality as they would in the office, and managers may request a return to office-based work if performance concerns arise.

The company provides a one-time home office setup stipend of $500 for employees approved for regular remote work. This stipend can be used for ergonomic equipment such as a desk, chair, monitor, keyboard, or other items that create a comfortable and productive workspace. Receipts must be submitted through the expense system within 60 days of purchase. Employees who already have an adequate home office setup are not required to spend the stipend. Additionally, the company will reimburse up to $50 per month for internet service costs upon submission of monthly receipts.

All remote workers must comply with our data security policies. This includes using company-approved VPN software when accessing internal systems, ensuring their home Wi-Fi network is password-protected with WPA3 or WPA2 encryption, and never using public Wi-Fi networks for work purposes without VPN protection. Confidential documents must not be printed at home unless absolutely necessary, and any printed materials must be securely shredded after use. Failure to comply with security requirements may result in revocation of remote work privileges.

Remote work arrangements are reviewed quarterly by managers to assess effectiveness. Factors considered include individual performance metrics, team collaboration quality, project delivery timelines, and employee wellbeing. The company reserves the right to modify or revoke remote work arrangements with reasonable notice if business needs change significantly. Employees will be given at least four weeks notice of any permanent changes to their remote work arrangement.

Parental Leave

We believe that welcoming a new child into your family is one of life's most important events, and we are committed to supporting our employees through this transition. Our parental leave policy is designed to give new parents meaningful time to bond with their child while providing job security and peace of mind during their absence from work.

Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. This leave can be taken all at once starting from the birth or adoption date, or it can be split into two separate periods within the first 12 months. For example, a primary caregiver might take 12 weeks immediately following the birth and reserve 4 weeks for later in the child's first year. Any split arrangement must be agreed upon with your manager and HR before the leave begins.

Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Similar to primary caregiver leave, this can be taken all at once or split into two periods within the first 12 months following the birth or adoption. Secondary caregivers must take at least 2 consecutive weeks for their first period of leave. Many secondary caregivers choose to align their leave with the primary caregiver's return to work, though this is not required.

To apply for parental leave, notify your HR representative at least 8 weeks before your expected start date whenever possible. For adoption, notification should occur as soon as the adoption timeline becomes reasonably certain. Your HR representative will provide you with the necessary paperwork and discuss your leave plan, including planned dates, communication preferences during leave, and arrangements for covering your responsibilities while you are away.

During parental leave, all benefits continue unchanged, including health insurance, dental coverage, life insurance, and retirement plan contributions. The company will continue to make its matching contributions to your retirement plan based on your regular salary. You will also continue to accrue annual leave during your parental leave period. Your role and an equivalent position are guaranteed upon your return, and returning employees will not face any adverse career consequences for having taken parental leave.

If complications arise during pregnancy or the adoption process that require additional time away from work, employees may be eligible for extended leave under our medical leave policy or through short-term disability coverage. Please discuss your situation with HR as early as possible so we can help you navigate the available options and ensure continuity of your benefits and protections throughout any extended absence.

Professional Development

We are committed to fostering a culture of continuous learning and growth. Investing in our employees' professional development not only benefits individual careers but strengthens our organization as a whole. We provide resources, time, and financial support to help you develop new skills, deepen existing expertise, and advance your career in directions that are meaningful to you.

Each full-time employee receives an annual learning budget of $2,000 that can be used for professional development activities. This budget covers a wide range of learning opportunities including online courses, in-person workshops, professional conferences, certification programs, books, and subscriptions to learning platforms. Part-time employees receive a prorated budget based on their contracted hours. The learning budget resets on January 1 each year and does not carry over to the next calendar year.

To use your learning budget, submit a request through the HR portal with details about the activity, its relevance to your role or career development goals, and the total cost. Requests under $500 require only manager approval and are typically processed within 2 business days. Requests over $500 require both manager and department head approval and may take up to 5 business days. For conferences that involve travel, you can combine your learning budget with the standard travel expense policy for transportation and accommodation costs.

Time spent on approved learning activities during regular work hours counts as productive work time and does not need to be made up. We encourage employees to dedicate at least 4 hours per month to professional development, though this is a guideline rather than a strict requirement. For intensive programs such as multi-day courses or certification bootcamps, employees can take up to 5 additional paid learning days per year, subject to manager approval and team coverage requirements.

After completing a course, certification, or conference, we encourage employees to share key takeaways with their team through a brief presentation, written summary, or lunch-and-learn session. This knowledge sharing multiplies the value of individual learning across the organization and helps build a collaborative learning culture. Employees who earn new certifications will also have them recognized in our internal directory and may be eligible for a one-time certification bonus of $200-$500 depending on the certification level and relevance to their role.

Code of Conduct

Our code of conduct establishes the foundation for how we work together and interact with our customers, partners, and the broader community. Every employee is expected to understand and comply with these standards regardless of their role, seniority, or location. These principles are not merely aspirational statements but are enforceable expectations that guide our daily decisions and behaviors.

We are committed to maintaining a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and retaliation in any form. This includes but is not limited to behavior based on race, ethnicity, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, religion, disability, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic. Harassment includes unwelcome conduct that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment, whether it occurs in person, over phone or video, through email or messaging, or on social media in a work-related context.

All employees are expected to maintain the highest standards of professional integrity in their work. This means being honest in communications with colleagues and clients, meeting commitments and deadlines or communicating proactively when they cannot be met, respecting intellectual property and confidential information, and avoiding conflicts of interest. If you are ever uncertain whether a particular situation presents a conflict of interest, consult with your manager or the legal team before proceeding.

Compliance with company security policies is mandatory for all employees. This includes following password management requirements, completing required security training on time, reporting suspected security incidents immediately, and properly handling confidential data according to its classification level. Employees who handle customer data must complete additional data privacy training annually and must not access customer records unless they have a legitimate business need to do so.

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this code of conduct, you have multiple reporting options. You can speak directly with your manager, contact your HR business partner, or use our anonymous ethics hotline at 1-800-555-ETHICS. All reports are taken seriously and investigated promptly and confidentially. We maintain a strict non-retaliation policy, meaning that no adverse action will be taken against anyone who reports a concern in good faith, regardless of the outcome of the investigation. Violations of the code of conduct may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.
