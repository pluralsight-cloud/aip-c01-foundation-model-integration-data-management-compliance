Name: Bob Marinelli
Staff ID: BP-STAFF-001
Role: Founder and Managing Director
Location: Head Office, Bristol
Contact: bob.marinelli@bobspizza.example.com
================================================================

Bob Marinelli founded Bob's Pizza in Bristol in 2003 after
spending ten years working in his family's restaurant in Naples,
Italy. He returned to the UK with a vision to bring authentic
Neapolitan pizza to British high streets at an accessible price
point without compromising on quality or ingredients.

Bob Marinelli oversees the strategic direction of the entire
Bob's Pizza business, including franchise growth, brand
development, and supplier relationships. He remains closely
involved in menu development and personally approves all new
product launches before they reach customers across the network.

Outside work Bob Marinelli is a keen cyclist and supports
several local Bristol food bank initiatives through the Bob's
Pizza community programme, which donates surplus ingredients
to local charities every week. Bob Marinelli is a firm believer
that great pizza starts with great flour and holds a monthly
open door session at Head Office where any member of staff or
franchise owner can raise ideas, concerns, or suggestions.

Name: Sandra Okafor
Staff ID: BP-STAFF-002
Role: Head of Food Safety and Compliance
Location: Head Office, Bristol
Contact: s.okafor@bobspizza.example.com
================================================================

Sandra Okafor joined Bob's Pizza in 2018 after a fifteen-year
career in food safety inspection with the Food Standards Agency.
She holds a Level 4 Award in Food Safety Management and is a
qualified HACCP auditor with extensive experience across food
manufacturing and hospitality sectors throughout the UK.

Sandra Okafor is responsible for the allergen management
framework across all Bob's Pizza franchise locations. She
conducts regular unannounced audits, maintains the product
specification sheets, and provides allergen awareness training
to all new kitchen staff and franchise managers joining the
Bob's Pizza network across every region.

Sandra Okafor led the implementation of Bob's Pizza compliance
with Natasha's Law in 2021. She represents Bob's Pizza on the
Food Standards Agency stakeholder advisory panel and regularly
contributes to industry working groups on allergen labelling
best practice. She is a sought-after speaker at food safety
conferences and has presented at events in the UK and Europe.

Location: Bristol Central

James Whitfield has worked in franchise management for over
twelve years, spending the first eight of those with a national
coffee chain before joining Bob's Pizza in 2020. He manages
all franchise locations across the South West region, covering
Bristol, Bath, Exeter, and Taunton on a regular visit schedule.

James Whitfield visits each location in his region at least
twice a month, assessing performance against brand standards,
supporting franchise owners with operational challenges, and
identifying opportunities to improve customer experience and
profitability. He is known within the business for his direct
communication style and his ability to turn around underperforming
locations quickly and effectively.

James Whitfield is a strong advocate for investing in kitchen
staff development and has championed a Bob's Pizza apprenticeship
programme in the South West region that has so far supported
fourteen young people into permanent kitchen roles across the
franchise network. He is currently overseeing the opening of
two new franchise locations in Plymouth and Cheltenham.

Maria Rossi trained at a culinary institute in Rome before
moving to Bristol in 2015. She worked at two independent
Italian restaurants before joining Bob's Pizza in 2019,
where she quickly became one of the most respected Head Chefs
in the entire Bob's Pizza network across all regions.

Maria Rossi is renowned within Bob's Pizza for her mastery
of Neapolitan dough technique and her meticulous approach to
allergen management. Under her leadership the Bristol Central
kitchen has achieved the highest food hygiene rating for five
consecutive years and has become a benchmark location for
training new Head Chefs joining the Bob's Pizza network.

Maria Rossi was named Bob's Pizza Head Chef of the Year in
both 2022 and 2024. She mentors junior kitchen staff and has
developed a popular internal training module on Italian dough
fermentation that is now used across all Bob's Pizza franchise
locations. She is currently developing a new sourdough base
variant that may be added to the menu in 2027.
